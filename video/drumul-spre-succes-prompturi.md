## Cadre

Wet asphalt mirrors the warm golden streetlights and the casino facade. Low-angle tracking shot at bumper height moves alongside the car, with puddle reflections rippling under the tires and light mist in the air. The car's matte-black body catches sharp highlights.

2. Slow push-in dolly from behind. City lights reflect faintly in the glass, soft rim light outlines the silhouette, and haze glows over the city.

   - 3c. A hand places a black Lamborghini car key on a glass table, slow motion, reflections, bokeh city lights behind.
   - 3d. Slow dolly toward a modern glass-and-concrete mansion entrance at dusk, warm interior light spilling out.

4. Coasta la apus (0:13–0:19): A black Lamborghini Urus speeds along a winding cliffside coastal road at golden-hour sunset above the Mediterranean. Dynamic tracking shot from a chase vehicle beside the car, then a dramatic slow-motion moment as it rounds a curve, with sun flare behind it, heat shimmer, and dust kicking up from the tires. The road surface and wheels are physically accurate.

5. Vila din dronă (0:19–0:23): Cinematic drone shot slowly descending and orbiting a futuristic luxury mansion with cantilevered concrete and glass, an infinity pool glowing turquoise, and pine forest and snow-capped mountains behind it at dusk. 6. Cadrul final (0:23–0:27): At night, a black Lamborghini Urus is parked in the illuminated driveway of a modern luxury mansion. Its headlights and LED daytime-running lights glow, with light beams cutting through faint fog. The camera slowly pulls back and rises in one smooth crane move, revealing the whole mansion against a starry sky. Calm, powerful, final.

## Montaj
- Tăieturi pe ritmul muzicii; match cuts: reflexia de pe asfalt → reflexia din geam; ceasul (3a) → cheia (3c).
- Tranziții: doar whip-pan și light leaks; speed ramp în slow motion la cadrul 4.
- Același LUT pe toate clipurile, granulație fină, benzi negre 2.39:1 în cadrul 9:16.
- Muzică (~26s): 0–8s drone grav · 8–13s percuție ticăit · 13–19s crescendo · 19–23s drop · 23–27s lovitura finală + reverb.
- Export: 1080×1920 sau 2160×3840, H.264/H.265, 30fps, fără text și fără watermark.
